Motor Controller & MPPT Interface PCBs

Sep 2025 – Jun 2026

Longhorn Racing Solar

  • 24V-to-12V power protection and thermal-management board that keeps the car's motor controller and MPPTs below thermal shutdown. Built 5 hot-swappable units; none failed across all three FSGP 2026 race days.
  • Cooling firmware in embedded C on an STM32L4 with FreeRTOS: a CAN ISR feeds temperatures to a control task driving PWM fans, holding both subsystems under 55°C and forcing 100% duty after missed readings.
  • Traced dead fan outputs to a MOSFET gate threshold above the 3.3V drive and buck failures to a regulator run at its absolute-max input, then respun with logic-level FETs and higher-margin parts.

Low-Voltage Carrier Board in bring-up

Jun 2026 – Present

Longhorn Racing Solar

  • 4-layer KiCad board designed to supply 150W (12.5A at 12V) from the car's 120V, 5kWh pack to every low-voltage system, through a 92%-efficient isolated DC/DC with ideal-diode source switching.
  • Voltage, current, and power monitoring at four points using Hall-effect sensors, chosen over shunt resistors in a cost, size, and availability trade study, reporting system health over CAN.
  • STM32G4-controlled precharge that holds off the load until the bus charges, plus a CC/CV buck-boost charger for the supplemental pack with low-voltage cutoff.

Addressable LED Strip Controller PCB

Jun 2026 – Present

Personal project

  • USB-C controller built around an ESP32-S3 that drives WS2812B strips for color-changing and audio-reactive signs, carried through a second revision.
